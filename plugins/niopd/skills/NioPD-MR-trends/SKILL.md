# Market Trends Analysis Skill

This skill identifies and analyzes market trends to inform strategic decisions.

## Instructions

### Step 1: Define Scope
- What market/industry?
- What time horizon?
- What's the purpose?

### Step 2: Research Trends
Categories to explore:
- Technology trends
- Consumer behavior
- Regulatory changes
- Economic factors
- Competitive dynamics

### Step 3: Assess Each Trend
| Trend | Direction | Impact | Timeline | Certainty |
|-------|-----------|--------|----------|-----------|
| [Trend] | Growing/Declining | High/Med/Low | Near/Mid/Long | High/Med/Low |

### Step 4: Identify Implications
- What opportunities emerge?
- What threats arise?
- How should we respond?

### Step 5: Generate Report
**File path**: `02-reports/[YYYYMMDD]-market-trends-v0.md`
