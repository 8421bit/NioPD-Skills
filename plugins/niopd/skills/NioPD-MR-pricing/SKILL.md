# Pricing Analysis Skill

This skill analyzes market pricing and develops competitive pricing strategies.

## Theoretical Foundation

### Pricing Strategies
| Strategy | Description | When to Use |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Cost-Plus** | Cost + margin | Commodity products |
| **Value-Based** | Customer perceived value | Differentiated products |
| **Competitive** | Match/undercut market | Crowded markets |
| **Penetration** | Low to gain share | Market entry |
| **Premium** | High for exclusivity | Luxury/quality |
